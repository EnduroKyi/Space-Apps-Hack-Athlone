# Space-Apps-Hack-Athlone
Idea summary for Space Mission Game for HackAthlone 2026 sponsored by NASA Spacea

Problem: For many students, it could be hard for them to get to experiment with an entire mission from concept to operation. 

The Solution: Our game includes educational concepts in a fun and easy ways for children to interact with. They get to design, launch and complete missions while learning 
              about space exploration and science. 
              
Key Features: 3 Levels, Single Player or Multiple Player Mode

              Level-1 : Design the Spacecraft 
              
              Level-2: Completing Tasks by Collaborating with different players with each assigned roles
              
              Level-3: Space Exploration with challenges and learning about different planets in terms of atmosphere, landscape and survival stimulator
